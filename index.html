    A Summary of Who am I

    Hello!

    I'm Cyrin, a web developer who enjoys crafting thoughtful digital experiences through clean design and purposeful code. I believe a good website should be as intuitive as it is visually engaging, balancing creativity with functionality.

    Outside of development, I enjoy creative hobbies that allow me to build, explore, and appreciate thoughtful design. Whether I'm documenting places through photography, assembling detailed model kits, or simply observing the architecture and stories behind the places I visit, I find inspiration in craftsmanship and the small details that often go unnoticed.

    These experiences shape the way I approach web development. Valuing patience, intention, and the belief that great work is built one thoughtful detail at a time.
