    WHO AM I

    Knowing Oneself is Life's Eternal Homework

    - Felicia Day

    My Story

    My journey into web development began with a simple curiosity on how ideas become experiences on a screen. What started as learning to write code gradually became an appreciation for thoughtful design, usability, and the small details that shape how people interact with technology.

    Today, I enjoy creating websites that balance functionality with aesthetics. Every project is an opportunity to solve problems creatively while building experiences that feel intuitive, purposeful, and enjoyable to use.

    Exhibit 01

    My Philosophy

    I believe that good design isn't about adding more, it's about knowing what truly matters.

    Whether I'm writing code, refining a layout, or adjusting the smallest visual detail, I value clarity, consistency, and intention. I enjoy creating work that feels timeless rather than trendy, focusing on experiences that are simple, elegant, and built to last.

    How I Build

    My work sits at the intersection of structure and feeling where design decisions are not just visual, but intentional.

    I’m comfortable working across the full layout process, from structuring UI systems to translating designs into functional interfaces, using tools like Figma for visual alignment and Git for version control.

    But beyond the tools, I focus on how everything connects: performance, usability, and the subtle details that make an interface feel alive

    Beyond Development

    Outside of development, I enjoy creative hobbies that allow me to build, explore, and appreciate thoughtful design. Whether I'm documenting places through photography, assembling detailed model kits, or simply observing the architecture and stories behind the places I visit, I find inspiration in craftsmanship and the small details that often go unnoticed.

    These experiences shape the way I approach web development valuing patience, intention, and the belief that great work is built one thoughtful detail at a time.

    Moving Forward

    I'm continuously learning, creating, and refining my craft. Whether it's building meaningful digital experiences, exploring new places, or taking on creative challenges, I believe every project is another opportunity to grow.
